What's new

Every change to Filter, newest first. Search the list to find when a feature landed or a bug went away.

1.0.0 New September 2026
  • Scheduled time windows. Give any app as many windows as you need. Allow-windows let notifications through only while they're active; block-windows silence an app just for those hours and always win over an allow-window.
  • Named windows. Call them Sleep, Deep work or Weekend detox, switch each one on or off, pick the weekdays, or set a specific date range for a holiday. Times are minute-precise and wrap past midnight.
  • Per-app muting. Every installed app in one searchable list, with a "show system apps" toggle, a blocked-today count per row, and a shortcut into that app's own notification settings.
  • Per-channel muting. Silence a single notification channel and leave the rest of the app alone. Channels are learned as apps post on them, so the list reflects what actually reaches you.
  • Block All. A global switch that mutes everything immediately, plus its own set of scheduled windows for a nightly blackout that needs no per-app rules.
  • Offline notification history. Every notification is captured — muted ones included — with instant search across app, title and body, All / Muted / Saved tabs and per-app filter chips.
  • Swipe to save or delete. Swipe right to pin a notification past the 30-day auto-purge, swipe left to remove it, and undo either from the snackbar.
  • Home feed. A newest-first view of what Filter just caught, with a blocked-today counter, sorting and a clear-all action.
  • Biometric app lock. Fingerprint, face or device PIN gates the whole app at launch and on resume. Free for everyone — never behind Pro.
  • Four accent themes. Sky Blue, Violet, Emerald and Amber, all on the same dark-first surface.
  • Guided onboarding. A checklist that deep-links to Notification Access, the Android 13+ notification permission and the battery-optimisation exemption, so nothing fails silently.
  • Status notification. A quiet, badge-free notice while filtering is active, so you're never muted without knowing. Launcher badging is off by default and toggleable.
  • Optional Google sign-in. Link Pro to your Google account so it follows you to new devices, and with Pro, back up the apps you block so they come back automatically. Notification content is never uploaded.
  • Light on the battery. One local database, no ads. Rules live in memory so there's no polling loop and no background drain.
